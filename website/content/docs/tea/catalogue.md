The open-source projects this server publishes as TEA products in their own right.

They are not a different kind of object. They are served by the same handlers, under the
same schemas, and validated by the same conformance suite as the tenant catalogue. See
[the open-source conformance report](../conformance/). That is the point:
TEA records software, not ownership, and a consumer cannot tell from a response which
catalogue it is reading.
